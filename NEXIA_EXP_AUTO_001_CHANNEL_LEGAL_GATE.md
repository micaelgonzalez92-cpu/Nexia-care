## Verified Spain constraint

Official consolidated Spanish law (Ley 34/2002, LSSI, Article 21) prohibits advertising or promotional communications by email or equivalent electronic means when they have not previously been requested or expressly authorized by the recipient.

The statutory exception applies where there is a prior contractual relationship, the contact data was obtained lawfully, and the communication concerns the sender's own products/services similar to those previously contracted. A simple, free opt-out mechanism is also required in the permitted relationship-based scenario.

Sources:
- BOE consolidated Ley 34/2002, Article 21: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2002/07/11/34/con
- BOE Act text: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758
