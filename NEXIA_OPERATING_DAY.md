# NEXIA OPERATING DAY — INTERNAL WORK CAPACITY

Updated: 2026-10-05

NEXIA uses this as a repeatable internal workday structure when safe autonomous capacity exists. It does not mean the chat is continuously active between sessions.

## 08:00–08:45 — Control Tower
- Recover NEXIA_STATE.
- Check mission, experiment, Human Gate, blockers.
- Check capability changes.
- Identify highest-value safe action.

## 08:45–10:15 — Product & Experiment
- Regression checks.
- Improve diagnostic clarity.
- Maintain experiment protocol.
- Convert public evidence into testable hypotheses.

## 10:15–11:45 — Demand Radar
- Find new public problem signals.
- Verify model/problem fit.
- Rank by evidence, severity, recurrence and testability.
- Falsify weak candidates.

## 11:45–13:15 — Economics
- Unit economics.
- VAT/fees/shipping/returns.
- Acquisition assumptions.
- Break-even and downside cases.
- Reject opportunities that only look profitable before costs.

## 13:15–14:15 — Acquisition Engine
- Prepare individualized recruitment routes.
- Verify authorized channels.
- Reduce manual work.
- Never contact externally without authorization.

## 14:15–15:15 — NEXIA HQ
- Keep mission, economics, experiment, capabilities and queue legible.
- Remove decorative metrics that could be mistaken for evidence.
- Keep state-driven refresh working.

## 15:15–16:00 — Resilience & Automation
- Integrity checks.
- Documentation.
- Recovery paths.
- Automation review.
- Avoid duplicate scheduled workers.

## 16:00–16:30 — Learning Loop
- What changed?
- What was falsified?
- What became more reproducible?
- What is the next mission?
- Record only evidence-backed progress.

## Definition of a productive day
A day is productive when NEXIA has produced at least one of:
- new verified evidence,
- a validated or falsified hypothesis,
- a safe system improvement,
- a measurable reduction in manual work,
- a reproducible process,
- a stronger economic case,
- or a clearly justified discard.

Documents and UI complexity without one of these outcomes do not count as progress.
