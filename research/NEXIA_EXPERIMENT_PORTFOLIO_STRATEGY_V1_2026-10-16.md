## Portfolio promotion rule
A lane is promoted only with evidence on:
- real problem/demand or high system-capability value;
- acceptance-quality capability;
- viable unit economics or measurable system benefit;
- bounded human attention;
- repeatability;
- security/legal/privacy/license fit;
- reversible next step;
- preregistered pass/fail thresholds.

## Rotation rule
If a lane hits a predeclared falsifier or stalls behind a human/external gate while another lane has higher information value, reallocate attention without rewriting historical results.
