# EXP-AUTO-001 — Contra Acquisition Microtest V1

Status: DESIGNED_NOT_AUTHORIZED
Date: 2026-10-14
Purpose: test whether Contra can produce buyer-intent for the same EXP-AUTO-001 offer faster than waiting on Workana moderation, without cash spend and without misrepresenting experience.

## HIPÓTESIS
A truthful Contra Independent profile built from existing technical evidence, followed by a maximum of 6 matched buyer-initiated applications, can generate at least 1 qualified buyer response within 14 calendar days at EUR0 cash spend and bounded human attention.

The 4-hour attention ceiling is an EXPERIMENT LIMIT, not a permanent Nexia rule. It exists to cap information-acquisition cost before commercial signal exists.

## ACCIÓN — only if explicitly authorized by Kael
1. Create one free Contra Independent account.
2. Build a truthful public profile using exactly 4 existing technical work samples/case studies derived from executed Nexia tests/artifacts. Every sample must be labeled as technical/internal test evidence when it is not paid-client delivery.
3. Set a transparent rate/positioning consistent with the EUR300 Lead Intake Reliability Sprint; no fabricated testimonials, client names, production history or revenue claims.
4. Kael personally completes identity verification and wallet setup through Contra/Persona. Nexia never requests raw ID, passwords, codes, payment credentials or identity documents in chat/GitHub.
5. Search only buyer-initiated jobs materially matching n8n/business automation/lead intake/reliability/API integration.
6. Submit at most 6 applications total during the test. No cold DMs, no off-platform outreach, no unpaid bespoke sample work, no paid boosts, no Pro upgrade.
7. Track matched jobs, applications, qualified replies, calls/requests, paid pilot, cash spend and Human Attention Hours.

## COSTE Y HUMAN ATTENTION BUDGET
Cash-spend ceiling: EUR0.
No paid plan, boost, application purchase or external paid tool.
Identity/wallet setup may disclose regulated personal data to Contra/Persona and therefore requires direct Kael action.

Total acquisition Human Attention ceiling: 4h, split before execution into two non-interchangeable buckets:
- SETUP_REUSABLE: max 2h for account/profile setup, four samples, positioning, official KYC/wallet steps and baseline search configuration.
- ACQUISITION_VARIABLE: max 2h total for up to 6 matched applications, equivalent to max 20 minutes average human attention per submitted application.

Unused setup time does NOT expand the variable acquisition budget, and unused acquisition time does NOT justify polishing setup beyond 2h. Thresholds are not moved after observing results.

## MÉTRICAS
Primary conversion metric:
- qualified_buyer_responses / submitted_applications.

Attention metrics:
- setup_reusable_hours;
- acquisition_variable_minutes_total;
- acquisition_variable_minutes_per_application;
- total_acquisition_human_attention_hours = setup + variable;
- human_attention_hours_per_qualified_response;
- human_attention_hours_per_paid_or_accepted_pilot;
- revenue_per_acquisition_human_attention_hour, once revenue exists;
- contribution_per_acquisition_human_attention_hour, once contribution can be reconciled.

Commercial secondary metrics:
- genuinely matched jobs found;
- requests for call/details;
- price objections;
- paid-pilot conversion;
- platform/payout friction discovered.

A qualified response must show real buyer engagement with scope, price, timing or next step; auto-acknowledgements and spam do not count.

UNKNOWN/NOT INSTRUMENTED is never recorded as zero.

## ÉXITO
Commercial signal success if either occurs within 14 calendar days after the profile becomes operational:
- >=1 qualified buyer response within the first 6 matched applications; OR
- >=1 paid/accepted pilot >=EUR249 equivalent with positive expected contribution.

A paid pilot still must satisfy EXP-AUTO-001/O1 delivery gates before being counted as O1 success.

The 14-day acquisition window begins only after the profile is sufficiently operational to view/apply to matched jobs; setup time before that is measured separately and does not silently shorten or extend the window.

## FRACASO
Fail the microtest if any occurs:
- 0 qualified responses after 6 matched applications and 14 calendar days;
- fewer than 3 genuinely matched opportunities are available during the 14-day window after profile completion;
- SETUP_REUSABLE exceeds 2h before reaching an operational profile;
- ACQUISITION_VARIABLE exceeds 2h before a qualified response;
- average variable attention exceeds 20 minutes per submitted application once all completed applications are measured;
- account/platform requirements force cash spend, misleading experience claims, or materially excessive support/administration.

## KILL CRITERION
Stop immediately if:
- any mandatory cash spend is required to continue the test;
- a proposal requires fabricated paid-client/production experience;
- requested unpaid custom work exceeds a trivial clarification;
- safety/compliance/KYC cannot be completed by Kael through official surfaces;
- platform mechanics make the EUR300 bounded pilot economically non-viable after current fees;
- 6 applications are consumed without a qualified response;
- either precommitted human-attention bucket is exhausted before its required milestone.

## INTERPRETATION RULES
- Setup time is a real cost and is never ignored in first-test economics.
- Because setup may be reusable, future repeatability analysis must also report marginal acquisition attention separately from total first-test attention.
- A positive first sale does not automatically prove the channel scalable; repeatability requires falling or stable marginal acquisition attention across later accepted pilots.
- A poor result cannot be rescued by retroactively increasing the 2h/2h/4h ceilings or application cap.
- A strong buyer response before using all 6 applications permits stopping early and evaluating the economic gate instead of spending the remaining attention budget.

## RIESGO
Main risks: public-profile exposure, identity/KYC data disclosure to Contra/Persona, weak new-profile social proof, hidden payout/FX friction, attention diversion from Workana.
Controls: one account only, EUR0 spend, six-application cap, no cold outreach, no false claims, 2h setup cap, 2h variable acquisition cap, Workana remains primary if approved.

## APRENDIZAJE ESPERADO
Determine whether Contra provides a viable buyer-acquisition channel for EXP-AUTO-001 with lower platform friction than Workana and without paid acquisition, while separately measuring reusable onboarding cost and marginal acquisition effort.

The result should answer:
1. Can the channel generate buyer intent at EUR0 cash spend?
2. How much one-time setup attention is required?
3. How much marginal human attention is required per application and per qualified response?
4. Does an accepted pilot preserve positive contribution after acquisition attention is included?
5. Is the channel worth a second repeatability experiment?

The result informs channel allocation; it does not create a separate business vein.
