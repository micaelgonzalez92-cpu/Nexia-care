## What stronger profiles do better
1. Cover sells the result before explaining the implementation.
2. One dominant message, often a bold transformation or metric.
3. Custom-designed visual language rather than exported screenshots/logs.
4. Strong negative space and hierarchy.
5. Cohesive portfolio identity without making every project look identical.
6. Rich media: video, mockups, photography, motion or polished illustrative systems.
7. Case-study storytelling extends beyond the hero image.
8. Technical stack supports the narrative instead of dominating it.
