## 1 — Contra
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Independent onboarding is available without a mandatory Pro purchase.
- To complete an Independent profile and become discoverable/apply to matched jobs, Contra requires a profile photo/cover information, four work samples, rate, social link, identity verification and wallet setup.
- Identity/wallet verification is handled by Persona and requires personal/ID/payment information. This is a Human Gate and Nexia must never request raw identity documents or secrets in chat.
- Contra has a Jobs feed with Apply/Dismiss flow and dedicated n8n discovery/category surfaces.
- Current official application documentation describes direct Apply/Dismiss flow; no Connect-style proposal token or mandatory application purchase was identified in the reviewed official flow.
- Free-plan platform fee for paid transactions is currently USD15 for USD1–499 and USD29 for USD500+; processor/payout and currency-conversion fees also apply.
- For SEPA/EUR local-bank payout, current official fee table lists USD1 local-transfer fee plus 0.40% EUR FX conversion fee; exact settlement economics must be recalculated at contract time.
- Contra funds are denominated in USD before non-USD payout conversion.
- Current public Contra evidence confirms active n8n/automation supply and lead-intake work samples. Community evidence also suggests automation/backend jobs exist but may be less visible than design/video categories; this is signal, not proof of sufficient buyer volume for Nexia.

Indicative EUR300-equivalent economics (ESTIMATE; exchange rate, tax and client-side fee allocation not fixed):
- Gross target: EUR300 equivalent
- Contra platform fee: approximately USD15 at this ticket size if covered by the Independent
- EUR payout friction: USD1 local-transfer fee + approximately 0.40% FX conversion, plus any applicable processor conditions
- Labor assumption: EUR60 for 4h delivery
- Expected contribution appears materially above the current Workana 20% fee assumption, but exact EUR contribution remains UNKNOWN until contract-time USD/EUR rate and fee allocation are known.

Strengths: no mandatory membership purchase identified, low fixed platform fee at O1 ticket size, direct job application flow, n8n category exists, strong long-term fit for portfolio/productized work.
Weaknesses: four public work samples, identity/KYC and wallet setup are required for a fully operational profile; new profiles have no platform earnings/reviews; automation demand may be less visible than creative demand.
Gate before activation: explicit Kael approval for account creation + public truthful profile + identity/wallet verification. Never imply paid-client production history.

## 2 — Freelancer.com
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Free members receive 6 bids/month, replenished approximately one every 120 hours.
- Bidding itself has no per-bid fee, but current official Fees & Charges requires a standard minimum available account balance of USD20 before placing any bid; higher risk-based/high-value balances may apply.
- Free trial of Plus requires a verified payment method and auto-renews unless cancelled; therefore it is explicitly excluded from the zero-spend experiment.
- Fixed-price freelancer project fee remains 10% of the winning bid or USD5 minimum.
- Current n8n demand exists, including lead-management automation requiring capture, qualification, notifications, duplicate prevention, testing, documentation and handover; competition can be high.

Economics at hypothetical EUR300 (ESTIMATE; taxes/FX/withdrawal excluded):
- Gross: EUR300
- Platform fee at 10%: EUR30
- After platform: EUR270
- Delivery labor assumption: EUR60
- Contribution before overhead/tax: EUR210

Decision: economically interesting but not a zero-cash test because USD20 must be held available to bid. Keep as reserve; do not fund an account without a separate spend/capital-exposure hypothesis and explicit Kael approval.
