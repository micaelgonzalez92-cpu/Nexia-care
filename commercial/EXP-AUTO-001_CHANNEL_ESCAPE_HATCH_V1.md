## 1 — Freelancer.com
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Free to sign up and place bids; fixed-price freelancer project fee is 10% of the winning bid or USD5 minimum.
- Bidding requires verified email, required skills, profile picture/headline/summary/hourly rate, language and location.
- KYC is recommended and can become required for compliance/transactions.
- Bid supply is limited by membership; extra 5 bids cost USD1, but no paid bids are authorized.
- Current demand signal exists for n8n lead/reliability work. A real-estate automation project posted 6 days before research requested lead validation, CRM/Airtable storage, assignment, notification, AI follow-up, reporting and documentation at EUR30–250; it completed with 139 proposals. Other n8n/lead-automation jobs show recurring market demand but often high competition.

Economics at a hypothetical EUR300 fixed-price pilot (ESTIMATE; taxes/FX/withdrawal costs excluded):
- Gross: EUR300
- Platform fee at 10%: EUR30
- After platform: EUR270
- Human labor assumption: EUR60 (4h x EUR15)
- Contribution before overhead/tax: EUR210
- This is EUR30 better than the current Workana 20% fee assumption, if all other costs are equal.

Main strengths: no published moderation queue before ordinary bidding, verified current n8n demand, free initial bidding path.
Main weaknesses: competition can be extreme, limited free bids, possible later KYC, no paid-production history must be misrepresented.
Gate before activation: Kael approval for a new account + truthful profile setup. Do not use paid membership, bid packs or verification upsells by default.

## 2 — Contra
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- A free Independent account is available.
- Standard onboarding is direct; to become discoverable/apply to matched jobs the profile must be completed with cover, four work samples, rate, social link, identity verification and wallet setup.
- Contra has a Jobs feed with Apply/Dismiss flow and dedicated n8n freelancer discovery pages.
- Free-plan platform fee is currently USD15 for transactions under USD500 and USD29 at USD500+; Pro waives Contra platform fees but is not authorized.
- Payout/processor and currency-conversion fees also apply and depend on method; Contra funds are paid in USD before non-USD payout conversion.

Indicative EUR300-equivalent economics (ESTIMATE only because USD fee/FX/payout cost is not fixed here):
- Contra platform fee roughly USD15 at this ticket size, plus processor/FX/payout cost.
- Likely platform friction is materially below a 20% Workana commission, but exact EUR contribution must be calculated at contract time.

Main strengths: low platform fee at O1 ticket size, no Connect-style per-proposal token found in the reviewed official application flow, n8n is an explicit marketplace category, strong longer-term fit for portfolio/productized work.
Main weaknesses: profile completion needs four public work samples and identity/wallet setup; these are account/publication actions requiring Kael. Current research verified category demand, not a specific matched live buyer for Nexia.
Gate before activation: explicit approval for account creation/public profile + truthful technical-work portfolio; do not imply paid-client delivery.

## 3 — Upwork
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Free to join on Freelancer Basic.
- Proposals consume Connects; Basic freelancers may receive 10 free Connects monthly, and additional Connects cost USD0.15 each with a 10 minimum purchase.
- Freelancer service fee varies from 0% to 15% per contract and is shown for the opportunity/contract.

Main strength: large market and potentially attractive fee range.
Main weakness: proposal-token economics introduce a variable acquisition cost and can incentivize spend before evidence. New account required.
Decision: research reserve; do not buy Connects or Plus.

## 4 — PeoplePerHour
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Freelancer applications are reviewed by a moderation team before selling.
- The current application guide requires email verification and a moderated application; payment/KYC verification can take up to 7 working days.
- 15 proposal credits/month are free.
- Service fee: 20% below EUR300 lifetime billing with a buyer; 7.5% from EUR300 to EUR6000; 3.5% above EUR6000, excluding VAT.

Interpretation: at exactly EUR300 it can be economically attractive, but it recreates part of Workana's moderation bottleneck and therefore is not the best escape hatch.

## 5 — Malt
VERIFIED public platform facts:
- Malt is strongly Europe-oriented and advertises more than 800,000 freelancers.
- Profile visibility requires a completed profile, availability, recent login and other quality criteria; minimum displayed daily rate is EUR100.
- Official material documents standard first-mission commission structures around 10% in supported markets, with lower rates for long client relationships / invited clients; Spain invited-client projects are documented at 2% excl. VAT.

Interpretation: strategically useful for European expansion and future direct-client administration, but weaker for immediate O1 because the current experiment seeks a fast buyer-initiated pilot rather than building another marketplace profile.
