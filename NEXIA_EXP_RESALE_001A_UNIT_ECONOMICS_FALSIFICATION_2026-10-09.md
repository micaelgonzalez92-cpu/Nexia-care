## Falsification result
The two thresholds are not jointly compatible at a €15 realized sale price if interpreted as an average contribution per unit under the canonical formula.

Before any packaging, channel, returns or preparation costs:
- At 10/10 received and sellable, €15 - €6.13 = €8.87 maximum contribution.
- At 8/10 sellable, acquisition cost per sellable unit becomes €61.30 / 8 = €7.6625; €15 - €7.6625 = €7.3375 maximum contribution.
- The minimum realized price needed to leave €10 contribution before other variable costs is €16.13 if all 10 units are sellable, or €17.6625 if only 8 units are sellable.
- Real-world required prices must be higher still to cover packaging, preparation, platform/channel costs and returns.

These are arithmetic scenario calculations from the verified checkout total, not realized results. No assumptions are made about tax treatment or platform fees.

## Interpretation
This is a metric-design inconsistency to resolve before declaring pilot success, not a reason to abandon the approved pilot. The pilot remains useful to measure actual sellable rate, realized price, preparation time, demand and contribution.
