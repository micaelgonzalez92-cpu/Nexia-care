## Why it can diversify Nexia

- recurring creative demand rather than implementation projects;
- e-commerce exposure aligns with the wider Nexia mission;
- deliverables can become modular: hook library, storyboard templates, character/voice presets, product-scene prompts, QA, multi-format exports.

- current Nexia has no verified client-performance portfolio in AI UGC;
- high-value buyers commonly ask for examples or ads that have already spent/converted;
- video generation/editing may require paid credits or subscriptions, so zero marginal cost is not established;
- quality consistency, product fidelity, hands/lip-sync and direct-response judgment create human QA burden;
- many marketplace listings are low-ticket, meaning demand alone does not prove attractive economics.

Do not claim profitability until one full creative batch has measured:
- generation/tool cost;

# VETA-AUTO-PRODUCT-001 — SELF-SERVE N8N RELIABILITY KIT / DIGITAL AUTOMATION PRODUCT

## Stage
VETA_CANDIDATE

## HECHOS VERIFICADOS
There is a current paid market for automation workflow packs and lead-intake/follow-up templates.

Observed Gumroad examples include:
- Lead Follow-Up Automation: USD79;
- Lead Capture & Enrichment Pack for n8n: USD25;
- n8n Workflow Starter Pack: USD29;
- Small Business Automation Starter Pack: USD97;
- six production-ready n8n workflows: USD80;
- a 706-workflow curated n8n library reports 116 sales.

Strong negative evidence also exists:
- generic workflow bundles are heavily commoditized, with products around USD1.99-10 and enormous bulk packs;
- generic Etsy business templates show 5,000+ listings and many best-selling products priced roughly USD1-15.

Therefore generic `n8n templates` or generic Canva/Excel templates are NOT the selected opportunity. Differentiation must be outcome-specific and reliability-focused.

## Nexia-specific product thesis
Turn the already verified technical assets behind EXP-AUTO-001 into a self-serve `Lead Intake Reliability Kit`, potentially including:
- importable n8n workflow;
- validation/normalization logic;
- persistent dedup/idempotency pattern;
- sample payloads and fixtures;
- safe human-review-before-send pattern;
- failure logging/alerts;
- setup/config template;
- QA/smoke-test checklist;
- handover/troubleshooting guide.

This is materially stronger than a raw workflow JSON because Nexia has already verified n8n 2.42.6 runtime execution and native Data Table persistence across process restart. That evidence must be represented honestly as technical verification, not paid-client production proof.

## Channel economics
Gumroad official current fees:
- direct website sale: 10% + USD0.50, plus payment processing (currently 2.9% + USD0.30 for card processing stated by Gumroad);
- Gumroad Discover marketplace sale: 30% inclusive of processing;
- no monthly fee.

Generic Etsy is not preferred for first validation because it has listing/shop fees and severe template commoditization.

## Opportunity thesis
Use a low-touch digital product to monetize the same proven capability repeatedly with near-zero delivery labor, then upsell optional bounded setup/customization services. This diversifies revenue away from pure project work while reusing engineering assets.

## Main falsifiers / risks
- demand for templates is real but price competition is severe;
- a product without distribution may make zero sales even if technically excellent;
- self-serve buyers can create support burden that destroys margin;
- customers may lack n8n/API skill, creating setup friction;
- n8n/version/API changes create maintenance obligations.

## Economics status
Promising but NOT VALIDATED.
At illustrative direct-sale prices of roughly USD49-79, platform/payment fees leave a plausible contribution margin before tax if support remains bounded, but Nexia has zero own sales evidence and must not infer conversion from competitor listings.

## Zero-cost next experiment
Before opening a store or publishing:
1. define one outcome-specific SKU only: Lead Intake Reliability Kit;
2. compare its feature/evidence matrix against at least 10 paid competitors;
3. build the minimum internal product package only from already verified/reusable assets if this requires no new paid tooling;
4. pre-register price tests and support limits;
5. identify one zero-cost or already-authorized demand test that does not require publication/contact; if no such test exists, prepare the exact Human Gate for a store/listing launch rather than publishing automatically.

## Preliminary success threshold
Promote to VALIDATION_CANDIDATE if:
- product has a defensible reliability/QA differentiation not present in most low-price packs;
- target price >=USD49 equivalent remains credible against current market examples;
- estimated setup/support <=30 human minutes median per buyer;
- gross margin before tax >=70% excluding one-time product-build work;
- maintenance can be bounded/versioned.

## Failure threshold
Deprioritize if:
- buyers treat the product as interchangeable with USD2-10 bulk packs;
- support >1h/buyer is required to make it work;
- the only viable differentiation requires live managed service, collapsing it back into EXP-AUTO-001;
- acquisition requires paid ads before any organic/marketplace signal can be measured.
