Historical/precommitted experiment gates remain unchanged.

## Purpose
Rank opportunities, capabilities and strategic work under **System Excellence + External Reality** without reducing every decision to short-term monetization.

## Hard gates
Reject/hold work that:
- violates law, security, privacy, platform rules or Kael authority;
- requires fabricated capability/evidence/results;
- creates material external effect without authority;
- cannot define the intended system/reality outcome and evidence needed;
- creates uncontrolled WIP or hidden maintenance without a compelling reason;
- requires major irreversible build before its core assumptions can be tested more cheaply.

Commercial candidates additionally require a plausible user/problem/channel or a justified discovery path before major investment.

## Two-stage scoring
### Stage 1 — Hard precedence
Any CRITICAL issue in safety/legal/security/privacy, truth/evidence, authority or continuity/recovery overrides normal ranking until contained.

### Stage 2 — Multi-axis value
Score each relevant dimension 1–5. Weights are chosen **before** comparing candidates when a formal numeric ranking is needed. Do not invent one universal weighting for every problem.

Dimensions:
- System criticality / blast radius
- Dependency centrality
- User/customer usefulness
- Strategic leverage / reuse / optionality
- Reliability / recovery / observability gain
- Competitive / professional quality gap closed
- External-reality / information value
- Human attention / toil reduction
- Economic sustainability / capital efficiency
- Urgency / time sensitivity
- Cost / reversibility / downside
- Maintainability / dependency burden

For a business opportunity, demand, willingness to pay, unit economics and time-to-cash can receive high weights. For recovery infrastructure, truth/reliability/dependency may dominate. For a public flagship asset, quality/user trust/competitive gap may dominate.

## Candidate classes
### Business / revenue engine
Check real problem, demand/payment evidence, contribution economics, delivery burden, recurrence, acquisition/distribution, support, platform dependency and scalability.

### System capability
Check blast radius, reuse, failure reduction, recovery, observability, maintenance, autonomy gain, human attention saved and whether external/reality evidence supports the need.

### Research / intelligence
Check decision unlocked, assumption falsified, freshness, source quality, information gain, reuse and expiry.

### Public / creative asset
Check intended audience/outcome, benchmark quality, proof integrity, legibility/usability, trust, differentiation and maintenance.

### Automation
Check baseline toil/error/risk, observability, idempotency/recovery, maintenance cost and measured net value after deployment.

## WIP promotion rule
Default material WIP max: 3.
Prefer when useful:
- one critical System Loop stream;
- one Reality Loop stream;
- one strategic/research option.

A new stream must justify displacement/addition through hard precedence or materially higher multi-axis value. Revenue proximity alone is insufficient.

## Promotion / kill
Promote when evidence materially improves system value, external usefulness, strategic leverage, quality, reliability, learning or sustainable economics enough to justify WIP.

Pause/kill when:
- predefined experiment failure occurs;
- assumptions are contradicted;
- information value collapses;
- maintenance/dependency burden exceeds value;
- a stronger system/reality bottleneck dominates;
- work becomes polishing without meaningful readiness/quality gain.

Sunk cost never changes thresholds.
