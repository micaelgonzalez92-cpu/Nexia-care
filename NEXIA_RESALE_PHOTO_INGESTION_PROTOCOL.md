# NEXIA Resale — Photo Ingestion & Item Matching Protocol

## Objective
Convert Kael's future photo dump into a verified mapping between images and inventory SKUs without inventing identity, model, condition or authenticity.

## Input
- Kael may upload all photos in one batch.
- Existing conversational inventory currently covers OWN-001 through OWN-017.
- Photos are evidence; prior verbal descriptions remain the baseline record unless contradicted by visible evidence.

## Matching order
1. Exact visible brand/logo.
2. Garment/accessory type and distinctive design.
3. Colour/pattern.
4. Size/label.
5. Unique defects or wear already recorded.
6. Product/model/reference codes when visible.
7. Only then assign the photo set to an OWN SKU.

## Ambiguity rule
If a photo cannot be mapped with high confidence, Nexia must ask Kael:
"¿Esta foto corresponde a OWN-XXX o OWN-YYY?"
Never silently assign an ambiguous photo.

## Photo set handling
For each SKU:
- front
- back
- labels/size
- brand/logo
- material/care label where useful
- closure/zip/adjustment
- defects/wear
- distinctive details
- product code/reference when visible

## Evidence states
- VERIFIED: image clearly supports the field.
- DECLARED: supplied by Kael but not visually confirmed.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence.
- CONFLICT: photo contradicts previous record; stop valuation until resolved.

## After matching
Produce:
- confirmed SKU ↔ photo mapping
- corrected description if visual evidence warrants it
- condition/QC
- model/reference identification candidates
- search terms for market comps
- pricing hypothesis
- listing draft
- missing evidence checklist

## Critical separation
Asking prices are not realized sales. Product retail price is not resale value. Brand recognition is not proof of premium resale value. Historical purchase price is not current market value.

## No-spend rule
Photo ingestion, matching, identification research and listing preparation are €0 activities. No purchase, subscription, paid database or external contact is authorized by this protocol.
