## Fresh scan — 2026-10-05

### Verified public signal A — ECAM22.110.B
Reddit r/Delonghidedica and r/DeLonghi contain the same public case from u/bdoru: a De'Longhi Magnifica S ECAM22.110.B began grinding about 30–40% less with unchanged settings/beans, producing weak coffee and irregular pucks; the owner reports service could not identify the problem. This corroborates the existing primary recruitment lead. It remains public signal, not NEXIA user evidence.

### Verified public signal B — Magnifica S leakage/flow
A public Magnifica S case reports diluted coffee, stopping halfway, and hot water being expelled below the machine. The owner had already tried the documented grind adjustment and hot-water step without success. This reinforces a boundary in EXP-001B: leakage/water-path cases require safety escalation rather than aggressive troubleshooting.

### Verified public signal C — repeated short-grind pattern
A long-running public Magnifica S discussion contains multiple reports of unusually short grinding and watery coffee, including an Aug 2026 follow-up reporting a prior workaround for reduced grind time. This is useful pattern evidence, but not validation of any NEXIA intervention.

## Decision

- Do not expand product scope based on public signals alone.
- Keep leakage cases as safety-sensitive secondary research.
- Treat all public cases as hypotheses/evidence of demand/problem existence, never as NEXIA outcome evidence.
