### Pilot interpretation
SUCCESS SIGNALS:
- >=80% sellable
- average realized sale price >=€15
- preparation time measured for every item
- contribution measured from real completed outcomes
- no repeated quality/compliance problem

FAILURE SIGNALS:
- <60% sellable
- average realized sale price <€12
- >20 min preparation per item without compensating economics
- repeated quality/compliance issues

IMPORTANT:
- Asking price is not revenue.
- Supplier resale claims are not evidence of realized sales.
- The pilot is not declared profitable until realized sales and variable costs are recorded.
- Scaling requires reproducibility across repeated batches.
