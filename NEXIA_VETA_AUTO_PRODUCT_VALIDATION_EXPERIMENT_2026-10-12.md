Product: `Lead Intake Reliability Kit for n8n`

## Purpose
Validate willingness to pay for a specialized n8n reliability product without confusing downloads, page views or technical completion with commercial validation.

## Hypothesis
Technically capable n8n users will pay at least USD 49 one-time for a focused reliability kit that reduces malformed/duplicate inbound lead handling and keeps ambiguous/customer-facing actions under human review.

## Product boundary
The paid SKU is a downloadable kit for the buyer's own n8n environment. It does not include hosting, CRM migration, WhatsApp setup, custom implementation or autonomous customer messaging.

## Price locked for first paid cohort
`USD 49 one-time`

Do not lower this price during the first cohort to manufacture success. A later experiment may test a different price only after recording the first result.

## Primary distribution hypothesis
Native n8n Creator Hub is strategically attractive because discovery is intent-aligned, but paid-template eligibility is gated behind 3 approved templates for a verified creator.

Proposed free creator ladder:
1. `Reliable Lead Intake — Normalize + Human Review`
2. `Persistent Lead Dedup Guard with n8n Data Tables`
3. `Authenticated Webhook Intake + Error-Safe Validation`

After 3 approvals, the paid kit can combine reliability controls, samples, QA, docs and self-serve installation as a differentiated product.

Account creation/submission/publication is a Human Gate and is NOT authorized by this document.

## Secondary distribution hypothesis
A direct storefront (e.g. Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad) can deliver the paid kit, but opening/publishing a storefront and accepting payments is a separate Human Gate. No account creation or publication is authorized.

## Metrics
Track separately:
- qualified product-page visits;
- free-template installs/downloads;
- paid checkout starts if instrumented;
- paid orders;
- settled paid orders;
- refunds;
- support minutes per paid sale;
- platform/payment fees;
- human maintenance minutes per month;
- realized contribution before tax;
- reconciled after-tax net profit when available.

## Initial success gate
Commercial validation requires ALL:
- >=5 settled paid sales at >=USD39 equivalent, with first cohort offered at USD49;
- refund rate <=10%;
- median support <=20 minutes per paid sale;
- positive contribution after platform/payment fees and support labor;
- no critical installation/data-loss/security defect.

## Early positive signal — NOT validation
Useful evidence but insufficient alone:
- one or more paid orders;
- repeat/referral purchase;
- multiple qualified users completing self-serve installation;
- buyer requests for vertical variants or implementation help.

## Failure / pause criteria
Pause or rework the SKU if any occurs:
- 0 paid sales after 300 qualified product-page visits, provided visit quality is instrumented;
- 0 paid sales after 100 free-template users reach a relevant paid-kit CTA, if that funnel is instrumented;
- refund rate >10% after at least 5 paid sales;
- median support >20 minutes/sale after at least 5 paid sales;
- installation cannot be completed by competent external users in <=30 minutes from docs;
- critical defect, unsafe default or licensing issue;
- market feedback consistently values bespoke implementation rather than the self-serve kit, in which case route evidence back to EXP-AUTO/service productization rather than forcing digital-product fit.

Do not apply traffic thresholds unless the traffic source and qualification are actually instrumented. UNKNOWN traffic is not zero and not evidence of failure.

## Economics screening at USD49
Do not treat platform-fee arithmetic as realized profit.

Screening expectation:
- variable digital COGS should be near zero;
- meaningful variable cost is platform/payment fee plus support labor/refunds;
- the main business risk is distribution volume and support burden, not production cost per copy.

To reach >EUR1,000/month after tax at this price will likely require dozens of monthly sales unless average order value rises through vertical packs/bundles or the product drives higher-value implementation. Exact sales requirement must use realized fees, FX, support and taxes after first sales exist.
