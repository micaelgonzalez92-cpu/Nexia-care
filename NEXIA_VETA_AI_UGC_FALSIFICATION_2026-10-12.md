## Hypothesis under test
A new AI-UGC/performance-creative provider can sell recurring e-commerce creative packages at >=EUR250 equivalent while keeping human work <=4 hours/package and contribution >=EUR150 before overhead/tax.

## Fresh buyer evidence
Current marketplace briefs confirm recurring demand for AI UGC/e-commerce ads using tools such as Higgsfield, Kling, Veo, HeyGen, Runway, ElevenLabs and similar stacks.

Observed price/competition examples during this scan:
- recurring AI UGC/avatar work around USD15-30 per video, with 10-15 proposals and portfolio examples required;
- several current e-commerce AI UGC briefs at USD10-50 fixed with 10-20 proposals and prior examples required;
- a higher-ticket USD630 ongoing e-commerce AI UGC brief exists, but had 20-50 proposals;
- a beauty starter batch at USD100 requested 2-3 concepts plus multiple hook variants, 1-2 revision passes and proven AI UGC track record;
- current Workana briefs confirm long-term AI-ad demand and in some cases provide paid generation tools, but still request demonstrated advertising/creative experience and real examples.

## Falsification result
The market-demand hypothesis survives, but the immediate extraction hypothesis is weakened.

Reasons:
1. Low-to-mid market price compression is severe on open marketplaces.
2. Strong buyers repeatedly ask for 2-5 portfolio examples or proven e-commerce ad work.
3. High-ticket opportunities exist but competition rises materially.
4. A proper package includes concept/hook work, generation, lip-sync/voice, edit, captions, product consistency and revisions; <=4 human hours is not yet demonstrated.
5. Generation-credit/tool cost and usable-output rate are not measured for NEXIA.
