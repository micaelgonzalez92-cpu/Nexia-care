## Opportunity A — Productized AI workflow improvement for small businesses

- Hypothesis: a narrow service that removes one measurable administrative bottleneck (enquiry triage, quote follow-up, document intake or weekly reporting) may create value without inventory or paid distribution.
- Evidence signal: OECD's 2026 D4SME survey reports growing SME AI adoption, while strategic/secure integration remains uneven. Its sample of 2,000+ SMEs across 12 OECD countries is non-representative and is not a Spain-specific demand estimate.
- Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/empowering-smes-in-the-age-of-ai_bf5a9816-en.html
- Zero-cost falsification: choose one workflow, build a synthetic-data demo, define a before/after metric and calculate delivery/maintenance time. Reject if benefit cannot be measured, native alternatives suffice, or safe data handling/support erases expected margin.
- Blockers: real customer evidence and external contact; no authorized contact route is connected. No prospects contacted.
- Economics: no validated price, conversion rate or delivery time.

## Opportunity B — Paid AI-content campaigns (Higgsfield Earn)

- Hypothesis: original campaign-compliant AI videos may earn a fixed campaign payout plus a performance bonus without first building a large audience.
- Evidence signal: official help describes campaign matching, required formats, guaranteed payout after submission and potential engagement bonus. Availability is regional and campaign-specific; social account connection is required. Public claims do not prove current Spain eligibility, available campaigns or actual payout.
- Sources: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-join-a-partnership-or-creator-program ; https://higgsfield.ai/es/earn
- Zero-cost falsification: inspect public campaign information only where accessible without login/account connection; draft 1–3 original concepts without paid generation credits. Reject if unapproved connection or paid credits are required, or terms/rights cannot be established.
- Blockers: account connection is a Human Gate; Spanish eligibility and live campaign rates are unverified.
- Economics: no income or acceptance claimed.

## Opportunity C — Digital templates/assets marketplace

- Hypothesis: a narrow digital product solving a repeated task could avoid inventory/shipping and allow repeatable delivery.
- Evidence signal: Etsy's official documentation lists a USD 0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee, plus payment processing and possible set-up/regulatory/advertising fees. Fees vary; marketplace access does not prove demand.
- Sources: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy ; https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/
- Zero-cost falsification: research demand and competing offers; draft a sample and calculate net proceeds at several prices including fees, taxes, support and creation labor. Do not publish or open/connect a shop in this pass.
- Blockers: listing and possible shop setup costs mean publishing is not a guaranteed €0 test; demand/conversion unknown.
- Economics: no sales or margin validated.
