# NEXIA Exception Desk — European validation decision

Date: 2026-10-05
Capital: EUR 0
External contacts: 0
Status: VETA PRIORITARIA — NOT VALIDATED

## Decision
Prioritize accounting/bookkeeping document-recovery operations over property maintenance and mortgage document collection for the first zero-cost commercial experiment.

## Evidence
- European accounting firms report capacity constraints and manual-process pressure.
- Public 2026 workflows repeatedly identify missing-document chasing as a recurring accounting bottleneck.
- Existing vendors prove willingness to pay, but generic document-chasing software is competitive.
- Property maintenance and mortgage document collection also have strong demand signals and established competitors.

## Strategic wedge
Do not build a generic SaaS. Test a human-approved managed workflow that compares expected vs received records, prepares missing-item follow-ups, tracks state, and escalates exceptions without accessing sensitive real documents during the first experiment.

## Next gate
Commercial validation with a real accounting/bookkeeping firm. No spend required. Until authorized external outreach is available, research and synthetic workflow design continue.
