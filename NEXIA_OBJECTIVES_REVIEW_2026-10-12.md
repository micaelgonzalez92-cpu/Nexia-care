## 3. Operating objective ladder

### O1 — First economic proof
Target evidence:
- >=1 paid pilot at >=EUR249 economic equivalent;
- payment settled;
- accepted delivery;
- <=4 measured human hours;
- positive realized contribution;
- zero critical unauthorized-send/data-loss/credential failures.

Promotion result: EXP-AUTO-001 may become VALIDATED only when all relevant evidence exists.

### O2 — Repeatability proof
Target evidence:
- >=3 accepted paid deliveries;
- median human_hours_per_accepted_delivery <=3h;
- positive realized contribution on each or on the reconciled cohort;
- no critical reliability failures;
- acquisition process repeatable without misleading claims.

Promotion result: REFINED candidate.

### O3 — Productization proof
Target evidence:
- >=5 accepted deliveries or equivalent external self-serve product uses with economically meaningful evidence;
- median human delivery time <=2h for service route;
- realized median price >=EUR400 or equivalent contribution economics;
- support burden measured;
- installation/onboarding reproducible.

Promotion result: PRODUCTIZED/EXTRACTION candidate depending on actual sales/cash evidence.

### O4 — Sustainable scale proof
Target evidence:
- rolling period with plausible and then realized route to >EUR1,000/month after-tax net profit;
- settled cash and full cost reconciliation;
- repeatable acquisition;
- bounded support/human hours;
- taxes, platform fees, refunds and overhead included rather than assumed away.

Promotion result: SCALABLE only with real economics.

## 4. Strategic objective split

Nexia should optimize simultaneously for three layers, in this order:

1. **Economic outcome:** settled contribution and after-tax net profit.
2. **Evidence velocity:** how quickly uncertainty is reduced per EUR and per human hour.
3. **Machine leverage:** measured reduction of human work through verified automation.

Machine leverage is subordinate to economics. Automation that does not reduce cost, risk, time or uncertainty is not strategic progress.

## 6. Stop conditions

Pause or discard a route when evidence shows one or more of:
- acquisition economics cannot support target contribution;
- delivery consistently exceeds time gate;
- competition requires misleading experience claims;
- tooling/support cost erases margin;
- legal/regulatory burden is disproportionate for the first proof;
- after a preregistered attempt budget, there is no meaningful buyer response.

Do not move thresholds after results arrive merely to manufacture a success.

## 7. Objective review cadence

Review objectives only when one of these occurs:
- paid pilot / settled transaction;
- experiment failure threshold reached;
- material change in available capital;
- new capability changes the feasible business model;
- legal/tax evidence changes economics;
- current North Star becomes demonstrably insufficient.

Do not rewrite objectives every week without new evidence.
