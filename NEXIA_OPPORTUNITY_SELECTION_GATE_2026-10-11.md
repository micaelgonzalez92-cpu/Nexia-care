# EXP-AUTO-001 — Lead Intake Reliability Sprint

Status: DESIGNED / NOT ACTIVATED EXTERNALLY

## Hypothesis
A narrowly scoped, reliable lead-intake automation can obtain at least one paid pilot at a price that supports Nexia's >€1,000/month net-profit objective better than low-ticket commodity listing work.

## Productized scope
One inbound lead source -> one Sheet/CRM destination -> duplicate prevention -> required-field validation -> immediate internal notification -> safe confirmation draft or approved confirmation step -> failure alert -> test evidence -> short handover document.

Default architecture must keep human approval for sensitive/outbound communication until explicitly authorized by the client. No invented data and no credentials requested in raw form.

## Price hypothesis
Target pilot price: **€249** one-time.

This is a hypothesis, not observed realized price for Nexia. It is intentionally above commodity micro-gigs because the revenue objective cannot be met sustainably by $5–$20 tasks.

## Unit-economics precommitment
Assumptions for the validation gate:
- realized pilot price: €249
- maximum measured delivery/review/support labor: 4.0 h
- internal labor assumption: €15/h = €60
- maximum attributable variable tooling/API/transaction allowance: €10 per pilot
- provisional contribution before acquisition overhead and taxes: €179 per pilot

At six identical pilots/month this would be €1,074 contribution before acquisition overhead and taxes. Therefore even a successful €249 pilot does **not** prove the >€1,000 after-tax objective; it only clears the next economics gate.

If real delivery time exceeds 4 h, real variable costs exceed €10, or realized price is materially below €249, recompute before continuing.

## Commercial validation gate
Maximum initial external sample after explicit authorization: 20 tightly matched buyer/prospect contacts or applications.

SUCCESS requires all of:
1. >=2 qualified buyer conversations/replies from the 20-contact maximum sample.
2. >=1 paid pilot at realized price >=€249 (or economically equivalent scope/rate).
3. Delivery completed in <=4 measured hours including review, correction and handover.
4. Zero critical data-loss, duplicate-send, unauthorized-send or credential-handling failures.
5. Buyer accepts the delivered workflow or agreed deliverable after the defined correction round.

FAILURE / PIVOT if any of:
- 0 paid pilots after 20 matched contacts/applications;
- market only supports prices that fail the contribution gate;
- delivery repeatedly requires >4 h at the target scope;
- paid tools/API/support requirements erase expected contribution;
- reliability cannot be demonstrated safely;
- buyer acquisition requires misleading portfolio/experience claims.
