# EXP-DOC-001 — Commercial experiment pack

Status: READY FOR HUMAN-GATED OUTREACH
Cost: EUR 0
External contacts sent: 0

## Hypothesis
A small accounting/bookkeeping firm will value a lightweight managed workflow that identifies missing client documents, prepares follow-ups, tracks responses and escalates exceptions without replacing its existing software.

## Offer
**Document Recovery Desk**

Input: expected-document checklist + received/missing status.
Output: prioritized missing-document queue, human-approved follow-up drafts, response/status log, exception list.

No software migration. No autonomous sending. No accounting/tax decisions.

## Pilot
- Synthetic dataset first.
- 10–20 example client files.
- Measure: time to produce missing-document queue; time to prepare follow-ups; percentage of records with a clear next action; exceptions identified.
- Target: demonstrate a repeatable workflow before any real client data is requested.

## Commercial hypothesis
Initial pilot should be positioned as a fixed-scope operational service rather than SaaS. Price is NOT validated and must not be presented as established market willingness-to-pay.

## Success
A real firm agrees to a bounded pilot and provides enough non-sensitive workflow information to measure time saved or backlog visibility.

## Failure
No meaningful time saving, too little recurring volume, existing software already solves the problem, or buyer will not provide access/workflow information even for a bounded pilot.

## Risk controls

- No financial/tax advice.
- No autonomous external messages.
