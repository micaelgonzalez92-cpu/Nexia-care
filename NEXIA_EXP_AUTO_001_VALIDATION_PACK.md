## Target customer archetype for direct validation

Prefer small service businesses where one missed inquiry can plausibly be worth materially more than the pilot fee and where the workflow is simple enough to bound.

Initial archetype filters:
- 2–20 person service business;
- receives inbound enquiries through one main web form/email channel;
- currently transfers lead data manually or has visibly simple disconnected tooling;
- one CRM/Sheet destination is sufficient for pilot scope;
- team benefits from faster acknowledgement/internal notification;
- no medical, legal, credit, employment-screening or other high-stakes automated decision domain for the initial pilot;
- no need for autonomous outbound messaging without explicit review;
- no complex migration or multi-system rebuild.

Example vertical archetypes for later public-prospect discovery:
- local home-service firms;
- small B2B agencies;
- training/education service providers;
- non-clinical appointment-based service businesses;
- small professional service teams where intake is administrative rather than regulated decision-making.

## NO-GO filters

Reject a prospect/job for the first pilot if any apply:
- requires false claims of prior client experience or portfolio;
- requires production-grade skill in a platform Nexia has not demonstrated;
- expected price is below the contribution gate without a strategic learning reason explicitly approved;
- needs paid software/API spend before buyer commitment;
- requires bulk migration, custom voice/telephony, regulated-data processing or autonomous high-impact decisions;
- requires raw passwords/secrets in chat;
- requires sending live customer messages before client approval and testing;
- scope cannot be bounded to one source and one destination;
- buyer requires long support obligations incompatible with the pilot economics.

## Truthful positioning

Do not say:
- "we have built this for many clients";
- "we are n8n/GoHighLevel experts" unless verified evidence later exists;
- "this will increase sales";
- "zero missed leads" as a guaranteed outcome;
- any invented portfolio metric.

Permitted factual positioning at current evidence level:
- Nexia has built and audited deterministic intake-classification prototypes with synthetic data;
- Nexia has implemented explicit duplicate/error/review guardrails in its internal prototypes and integrity tooling;
- the proposed pilot is intentionally narrow and measured;
- live sending/production activation occurs only after agreed tests and approval;
- the pilot is a validation engagement, not a claim of prior production deployment.

## Draft offer structure — for approval before sending

### Lead Intake Reliability Sprint — €249 pilot hypothesis

Deliverable:
1. map one inbound lead source and one Sheet/CRM destination;
2. define required fields and validation rules;
3. implement or configure capture/update logic within the agreed available stack;
4. prevent obvious duplicates/idempotency failures where technically supported;
5. create internal notification and safe acknowledgement step;
6. add failure visibility/logging appropriate to the stack;
7. test with sample leads before any live activation;
8. provide a concise handover document;
9. one bounded correction round.

Boundary:
- no paid third-party subscription/API purchased by Nexia without separate approval;
- no production activation before client acceptance of the test;
- no promise of revenue uplift;
- no regulated decision automation in the first pilot.
