# NEXIA — OPPORTUNITY SCORECARD V2

Status: ACTIVE FOR FUTURE CANDIDATE RANKING / DOES NOT RETROACTIVELY CHANGE EXP-AUTO-001 GATES
Date: 2026-10-14
Purpose: turn broad opportunity generation into disciplined selection without opening excessive WIP.

## 1. Funnel

A research pass may generate up to 30 candidates as a longlist. That is discovery only.

`Longlist <=30 → retain Top 10 → deep-review Top 3 → max 2 material research veins → 1 primary monetization experiment.`

A candidate cannot displace the primary experiment merely because its narrative is attractive. It must show materially better evidence-adjusted expected value or the current primary must fail its predefined gate.

## 2. Hard gates before scoring

Reject or hold any candidate that:
- violates law, platform policy, Nexia authority or security rules;
- requires unapproved spend, account creation, publication, contact or commitment to validate;
- depends on fabricated experience, testimonials, users, revenue or capability;
- lacks an identifiable buyer/problem/channel or a testable willingness-to-pay path;
- cannot define a minimum experiment with metric, success/failure and kill criterion;
- would create a third material parallel research vein;
- requires building a large asset before problem + buyer + channel + payment intent are evidenced.

## 3. Weighted score — 100 points

Scores are ESTIMATES from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) and must cite evidence/freshness where material.

| Dimension | Weight | What high score means |
|---|---:|---|
| Real demand / willingness-to-pay evidence | 20% | buyer-initiated demand, payments, hires, credible purchase intent; own evidence outranks public signals |
| Contribution / after-tax profit path | 20% | strong unit economics after variable costs and human labor, with plausible tax/overhead path |
| Time to first settled euro | 10% | short path from validation to real cash without hidden dependencies |
| Repeatability / natural recurrence | 10% | repeated transactions or recurring value without forcing subscription where value is one-time |
| Human attention / automation potential | 10% | low human attention after standardization; automation can be verified safely |
| Maintenance / support burden | 10% | low ongoing update/support/exception burden relative to economics |
| Scalability / defensibility | 10% | capacity can expand without proportionate human labor and has differentiation/data/process/channel advantages |
| Platform / supplier / channel dependency risk | 5% | low single-platform or single-supplier fragility; reversible channel exposure |
| Capital / downside / reversibility | 5% | low cash at risk, fast learning, easy stop/recovery |

Weighted total = sum(score/5 × weight).

## 4. Attention-light doctrine

Do not label a model superior merely because it is called “passive”. Score the actual operating burden.

Preferred metric once measurable:
- Revenue or contribution per Human Attention Hour.
- Human Attention Hours/week.
- Human Actions/transaction.
- Maintenance/support hours per customer or asset.
- Exception rate.

Recurring revenue, software, courses, templates, memberships, licensing and creator income can all be attractive, but none is assumed passive. Distribution, churn, customer support, content refresh, platform changes and technical maintenance count as real work/cost.

## 5. Model-specific checks

### Productized services
Check scope containment, delivery hours, support leakage, acquisition cost, reuse of components and productization path.

### Digital products / templates / licenses
Check buyer problem, differentiation, marketplace saturation, distribution channel, support/update burden, licensing/IP, refunds, platform fees and external-user installation/usability.

### SaaS / software
Check repeated problem, retention driver, hosting/support/security burden, churn risk, CAC/LTV only after real data, and whether ongoing value naturally justifies recurring billing.

### Content / audience / affiliate
Treat audience as a distribution asset, not a business result. Measure reach → lead → buyer → settled revenue → contribution → recurrence. Check platform eligibility, policy dependence, disclosure/KYC requirements and content-production attention.

### E-commerce physical
Check landed cost, VAT treatment, shipping, returns/defects, inventory/capital lock, CAC, sell-through, contribution and supplier concentration.

## 6. Promotion rule

Promote a research vein only when evidence improves at least one of: real demand, economics, controllability, repeatability, or time-to-cash enough to justify the added WIP.

Do not promote because of follower counts, market-size headlines, AI novelty, technical elegance or theoretical TAM alone.

## 7. Kill / pause rule

Pause or kill when a predefined failure gate is hit, economics break, evidence is stale/contradicted, maintenance destroys the attention-light thesis, acquisition requires misleading claims, or a stronger opportunity dominates after evidence-adjusted comparison.

Sunk cost never changes the threshold.

## 8. Current-state boundary

This scorecard improves future ranking only. It does not change EXP-AUTO-001 price, success criteria, Workana authorization, Human Gates or WIP limits. Current primary remains EXP-AUTO-001 until canonical evidence changes that decision.
